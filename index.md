Hello! I'm Yona B. Lapeyre, 3rd year PhD candidate at the Centre de Recherche Astrophysique de Lyon in France. My research focuses on warped accretion discs around a variety of objects, from singular black holes to multiple-star systems. I am one of the developers of Shamrock, an astrophysics code tailored to harness the power of modern, exascale supercomputers.

Before my PhD, I graduated from ENS de Lyon with a master's degree in theoretical physics. A full CV can be downloaded [here](https://y-lapeyre.github.io/downloads/cv.pdf).
