I am a member of the Lyon chapter of Astronomy on Tap. We organize monthly evenings of outreach, covering a wide array of topics from current works in general relativity to the latest 4MOST news. I gave one talk titled "A guide to firing cosmic shots", explaining the mechanisms of ejections of ultra-relativistic jets from black holes.


In collaboration with artist Alex Andrix (alexandrix.com), we produced both scientific and artistic visualizations from actual simulations.
