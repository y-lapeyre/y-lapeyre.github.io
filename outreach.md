## Astronomy On Tap
I am a member of the [Lyon chapter of Astronomy on Tap](https://astronomyontap.org/locations/lyon-france/). We organize monthly evenings of outreach. I gave several talks:

- A guide to firing cosmic shots explaining the mechanisms of ejections of ultra-relativistic jets from black holes.
- Voicing the dark: how do black holes speak to us ? on how astronomers gather information from dark objects.
- Astronews: a recap of the latest news the Astronomy/Astrophysics community!

## Pluridisciplinarity
I was invited by the Institute for the History of Representations and Ideas in Modernity (IHRIM) to introduce the concept of black holes to researchers in Philosophy and History of Arts at a workshop on [the colour black in modern thinking and creative process](https://ihrim.ens-lyon.fr/evenement/etude-noire-sur-fond-noir-de-la-couleur-noire-dans-la-creation-et-la-pensee).


## Artistic projects
In collaboration with artist Alex Andrix (alexandrix.com), we produced both scientific and artistic visualizations from actual simulations. One of these is now [displayed in the ENS]({{ '/assets/vernissage_fractured_rings.jpg' | relative_url }}).
